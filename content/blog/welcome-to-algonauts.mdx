Welcome to **Algonauts**, our portal for competitive programming!

Competitive programming is more than writing code; it's about solving hard logical problems under strict execution time and memory limits. Whether you are aiming for the ICPC, Google HashCode, or simply want to climb the rating steps on Codeforces, this club provides the ecosystem you need to grow.

### Our Training Model

Our activities are structured into three pillars:
1. **Weekly Contests:** Simulating ICPC / Codeforces rounds to practice solving under pressure.
2. **Interactive Workshops:** Peer-to-peer lessons breaking down advanced data structures and algorithms.
3. **Curated Resources:** Structured roadmaps matching rating levels so you always know what to solve next.

Stay tuned as we post regular editorials, contest post-mortems, and strategy guides here!
